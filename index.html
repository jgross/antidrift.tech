Five things we believe

  1. One session, one goal.

    A session that can't say what it's for isn't working. It's wandering. Every session in Antidrift belongs to a goal, and the goal has a "done when".

  2. Agents don't get tired. You do.

    Babysitting sixteen tabs is not a job. Antidrift watches every session and puts only the few that need you on one board: drifted, waiting on you, or probably done.

  3. "Done" means done.

    "I've implemented the feature!" is a claim, not a result. Goals carry criteria, and nothing gets closed until they're checked.

  4. Your Mac. Your code. Your business.

    No account. No cloud. No telemetry. Antidrift keeps everything in a SQLite file on your disk and talks to nothing but your own Claude sessions.

  5. Quit the app, keep the work.

    Every session lives in its own tmux server. Quit Antidrift, update it, crash it: your agents keep going. Attach from any terminal you like.

Why I built this

Three projects.
Fourteen goals.
One head.

I run three projects across more than one repo, with around fourteen goals open at any time and a Claude session or three on each.

Every morning I'd find a session that had gone off on a side quest, another that had been waiting on me since lunch, and a goal I'd forgotten I'd started. More agents made me faster. They didn't make me more focused.

So I built the tool I wanted. I use it every day. Now I want to know if you'd use it too.
